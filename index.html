About us

  • Authentic French style pastries using family recipes that have been passed down by four generations of French chefs. We use only the finest quality ingredients to produce the highest quality and best tasting products on the market.

    We have two lines of business – our Foodservice division produces frozen pre-proofed and egg washed croissants and pastries and our Retail division produces packaged frozen “Rise & Bake” pastries for sale through retail locations.
